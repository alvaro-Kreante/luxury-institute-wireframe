Five Pillars rubric · v1.3

Trustworthiness

Was the guest given accurate information, reliable follow-through and honest treatment?

Expertise

Did staff demonstrate competence and solve the need effectively?

Empathy

Did staff recognize and understand the guest's circumstances and feelings?

Kindness

Was care demonstrated through considerate behavior?

Creativity

Was there thoughtful initiative or an appropriate original solution?
